# Data & image credits

All data was fetched once from open-access sources and is stored locally in `deeptime/data.js`.

- **Fossil occurrences** — The Paleobiology Database, https://paleobiodb.org (CC BY 4.0)
- **Geologic time scale & colours** — Macrostrat, https://macrostrat.org (CC BY 4.0), after the International Commission on Stratigraphy
- **Gene alignments (human vs. zebrafish / spotted gar orthologues)** — Ensembl, https://rest.ensembl.org
- **Lineage of Homo sapiens** — Open Tree of Life, https://opentreeoflife.org
- **Silhouettes** — PhyloPic, https://www.phylopic.org
